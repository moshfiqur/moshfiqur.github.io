About Me

Over the last 12+ years I’ve lived and breathed computer science. After completing my undergraduate studies in Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering, I went on to complete a master degree in Master in Computer Science at the Technical University of Kaiserslautern.

Whilst studying, I worked for Fraunhofer Institute for Experimental Software Engineering. To complete my Master thesis, I paired up with the German Research Centre for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI), to explore the area of Semantic Desktop research.

Toward the end of my studies, I began working for Yalwa GmbH, a global e-commerce platform. After completing my master degree I accepted a position as a Software Development Specialist with the same company.

To see a system is being built from requirement engineering to deployment is my passion. Though I have successfully worked in different desktop and web application development projects, web is my favorite work place. I am interested in Social and Professional Networks, Big Data, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, IoT and creating services that make peoples lives more easier and connected.
